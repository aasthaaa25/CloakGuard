The advantage of knowing, and
acting, first

TrendAI™ ZDI is the starting point for many of the safeguards TrendAI™ customers rely on. TrendAI™ ZDI's research becomes proactive filters that narrow the window of exposure before attackers can exploit a flaw. Strengthened by more than 20 years of vendor partnerships, organizations gain earlier, faster, and broader security coverage than with any other provider.

  • Backed by more than 450 dedicated TrendAI™ researchers across 14 global threat centers and an external community of over 19,000 independent vulnerability researchers
  • Industry-leading visibility with 2.5x more disclosures than the next program
  • Filters deployed across IPS, endpoint, and cloud workload defenses
  • Direct mapping between TrendAI™ ZDI advisories and TrendAI Vision One™

Early visibility that makes
AI security smarter

AI is only as strong as the intelligence behind it. TrendAI™ ZDI gives strategic, actionable insights into vulnerabilities before they're made public: how they behave, how they break, and how attackers could exploit them. This foundation enables TrendAI Vision One™ to focus on what needs attention first long before risk turns into compromise, while continuously learning to adapt to emerging threats and real-world attacks.

  • Enhanced risk scoring that reflects attacker techniques
  • AI-driven prioritization informed by validated vulnerabilities
  • Data lineage that keeps AI-powered decisions explainable, auditable, and trustworthy for security teams
