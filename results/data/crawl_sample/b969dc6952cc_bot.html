Dunham, Lake Arthur Highlight Class 2A Basketball All-State Lists

March 30, 2021

Written for the LSWA

Carlos Stewart and Deonna Brister had storybook endings to their respective senior seasons of high school basketball.

Both players entertained fans with stellar play during memorable runs to LHSAA championships earlier this month. Now Brister and Stewart have been selected as the Outstanding Players on the LSWA’s Class 2A All-State teams.

Stewart led Dunham to a second straight Division III crown. The Santa Clara signee averaged 32.1 points a game, 5.2 rebounds, 4.3 assists and 3.8 steals. He made 44.5 percent of 3-pointers, 55.4 percent overall and 85.7 percent of his free throws.

Brister helped Lake Arthur win the Class 2A girls championship. The Nicholls signee averaged 19.8 points per game, 10.4 rebounds, 3.0 steals, 2.5 assists and 1.2 blocks per game. She had 18 points, seven rebounds and seven blocks in championship game win over Doyle.

Stewart and Brister were joined by their coaches on top of the Class 2A All-State team.

Dunham’s Jonathan Pixley was voted the Class 2A boys Coach of the Year and Vickie Sketoe netted the girls Coach of the Year honor.

Pixley has led Dunham (18-11 overall) to Division III titles in three of the last five seasons, while leading the Tigers to a fifth straight title games.

Sketoe led Lake Arthur (28-4) to its first LHSAA girls basketball title since 1976. LAHS was the 2A runner-up in 2018 and had been in the tourney four times in the last five years.

Stewart was joined on the Class 2A first-team by Rayville’s Kashie Natt (18.0 PPG), Franklin’s Travis Zeno (21.1 PPG), Port Allen’s Tawasky Johnson (15.0 PPG) and Newman’s Chris Lockett (19.0 PPG).

Joining Brister on the girls’ team were Rayville’s Amari West (26.0 PPG), Lafayette Christian’s Jada Richard (21.2 PPG), St. Thomas Aquinas’ Jade Brumfield (18.0 PPG) and Doyle’s Presleigh Scott (22.6 PPG).
