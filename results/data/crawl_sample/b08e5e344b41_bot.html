What is Mindtools membership?

Mindtools membership for Individuals gives you access to expert-led learning designed to build leadership, management and personal effectiveness skills — at your own pace and on your own terms.

You’ll get access to a wide range of content, including videos, articles, quizzes, Skill Bites (short learning journeys) and AI-powered coaching through Ask M: Coach.

Mindtools combines research-backed insights with practical, on-demand resources. Every course and article is designed to deliver real results you can apply immediately at work.
