Every month we will highlight a local nonprofit in or around the German Village area. 

The Boys & Girls Clubs of Columbus administrative headquarter is located at historic City Park Place on City Park Avenue and Thurman Avenue.  Here is the mission statement"

"Our mission is to empower young people to reach their full potential as productive, caring and responsible citizens."

I was surprised to find out Columbus youth stats are worse than many other locations in the United States.   Please see below and donate to their important cause.
