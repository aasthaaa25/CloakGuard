Who Are We?

God's abundant favor and mercy has made it possible for Biblestudy.org, now 30 years old, to be one of the oldest Bible based destinations preaching the gospel on the Internet!

In 2025 the site attracted 10.3 million visitors with its wide selection of materials. It contains 2,010+ articles including our own maps, charts, timelines and genealogies. In 2026 many more articles, as well as custom maps, are planned for the site!

The site has additionally made progress in creating its own Biblical commentary. This commentary, of which 31 books are now complete and freely available, offers brief chapter outlines, as well as questions and answers AND fascinating facts!

Sources and Support

All content is created and edited by the Webmaster who is the site's owner and sole operator. Our content is 100% created by humans and not A.I.!

This site uses quotes from a variety of translations in our materials such as the King James Version, New King James Version, New International Version and many others. We also often use the Holy Bible, a Faithful Version (HBFV) since we find it to be one of the most accurate translations of God's word.

Unlike countless other religious sites, we are 100% independent and have NEVER been sponsored, operated by or an outreach of any church or group. Our support comes from visitors like you! If we have helped you, please help us stay online and MAKE A DONATION! Thank you!

Our Motto

We encourage you to verify our content through prayer and Bible study. Do not believe us (or anyone else!) just because we (or they) say so. Believe your Bible!
