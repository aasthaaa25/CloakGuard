The Light: Swami Vivekananda Songs Lyrics

The Light: Swami Vivekananda Songs Lyrics & Videos: The Light: Swami Vivekananda is a 2013 Bollywood Hindi movie directed by Utpal Sinha and produced by Tricolour Productions Pvt. Ltd. under the banners Tricolour Productions Pvt. Ltd.. The film, starring Deep Bhattacharya, Sayak Chakraborty, Gargi Roy Choudhury, Premankur Chakraborty, Courtney Stephens, Biswajit Chakraborty, Piyali Mitra and Archita Sahu was released in theatres on 1 February 2013. The Light: Swami Vivekananda songs are composed by Nachiketa Chakraborty and Dr. Hari Charan Verma, while J. Misra, Goswami Tulsidas, Rabindranath Tagore, Traditional, Swami Vivekananda, Sant Kabir, Ajay Udayanath Pakrashi and Sant Kavi Surdas wrote its lyrics.
