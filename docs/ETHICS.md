# Crawl and evidence handling

Collect new data only from owner-authorized hosts or approved local fixtures. Record the authorization and sampling frame before scheduling captures. Honor robots.txt and documented rate limits, identify the research crawler with a contact address, and stop on challenges, rate limits, or withdrawn consent. Keep Chrome-like and bot-like profiles within that authorization. Do not impersonate verified Google infrastructure or bypass access controls.

Collect the minimum artifacts necessary for review. Keep raw evidence private with access restricted to reviewers. Remove cookies, credentials, personal information, and sensitive URL parameters from release copies. Set a retention deadline in the collection manifest and delete raw evidence after adjudication unless the owner authorizes retention. Use escaped source and extracted text for annotation.

Route suspected compromises to the site owner through a private disclosure process. Release aggregate results, approved fixtures, and redacted evidence. Keep live exploit details and identifying raw evidence private.
